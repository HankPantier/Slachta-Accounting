## LinkedIn

A dental practice owner in Hudson, Wisconsin sat down with our team last spring and found fourteen active software subscriptions on her business account. Three she didn't recognize. Two were tools her office manager trialed two years earlier and never canceled. Total cost: $612 a month, $7,344 a year, gone before she ever looked at a profit and loss statement.

This is subscription creep, and it shows up in nearly every conversation we have with dentists, attorneys, architects, and service-based businesses across Minnesota and Wisconsin. No single charge breaks a budget. The stack of them, left unreviewed for a year, absolutely can.

Our post walks through the 90-day audit process and the three-bucket sorting method that finds the savings fastest.

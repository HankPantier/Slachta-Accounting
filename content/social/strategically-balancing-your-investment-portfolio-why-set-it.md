## LinkedIn

A 60/40 stock-bond mix from 2015 can quietly become 75/25 today, with no decisions made and no alarms tripped. Ten years of a strong equity market will do that on its own.

For a dentist with a SEP-IRA and a taxable brokerage account, or an attorney juggling a 401(k) and outside investments, that drift usually happens in two places at once. Retirement accounts often rebalance automatically inside a target-date fund. Taxable accounts don't, and selling there means facing a capital gains bill tied directly to timing: your income year, your tax bracket, whether you're already itemizing.

That's the piece a financial advisor's rebalancing recommendation can miss and a CPA catches. We coordinate with clients' advisors on exactly this timing, so a trade in October doesn't collide with a Section 199A deduction or a fourth-quarter distribution in December.

## LinkedIn

A dentist in Stillwater asked us if she owed tax on $4,200 in business credit card cash back. Usually no. The IRS treats most rewards as a rebate, not income.

The bigger question is whether your card structure matches your actual spending. A dental practice spending $8,000 a month on supplies fits a different card than a service business with $3,000 in recurring software costs. Category-bonus cards, flat cash back, and travel points each pay off differently depending on where your money goes.

The businesses that get the most out of rewards are the ones with clean monthly books already. If yours are behind, that comes first.

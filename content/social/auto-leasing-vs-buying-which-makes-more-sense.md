## LinkedIn

A dentist in Hudson, Wisconsin asks us the same question every March: lease the new SUV, or buy it outright?

Here's the number that usually settles it. Buy a $70,000 SUV and use it 80% for practice-related travel, and Section 179 can generate a substantial first-year deduction. Lease that same vehicle and you're capped at 10,000-15,000 miles a year, with overage charges running 15 to 30 cents per mile if you go over.

Neither option is automatically better. It depends on how many miles you actually drive, how long you keep vehicles, and whether you need a big deduction this year or a steady one over several years. That's the framework we walk dentists, attorneys, and service-based business owners through before they sign anything.

## LinkedIn

A two-year nursing home stay in Minnesota or Wisconsin runs $180,000 to $240,000. A five-year stay can top $600,000. Medicare covers almost none of it, capping skilled nursing care at around 100 days.

We work with business owners, dentists, and attorneys who have built solid retirement accounts and real estate equity but have never run these numbers against their own plan. The right funding mix usually blends three sources: long-term care insurance, VA Aid and Attendance for eligible veterans, and self-funding from savings earmarked years in advance. Waiting until a fall or a diagnosis forces the decision removes every good option.

Our latest post breaks down what a private room actually costs in Minnesota and Wisconsin, how LTC insurance premiums compare to self-funding thresholds, and how VA benefits get missed by families who assume they don't qualify.

## LinkedIn

A dentist in Stillwater called us holding two life insurance policies, a disability policy she'd forgotten about, and an umbrella policy sized for a net worth she'd outgrown a decade ago. She was paying roughly $4,200 a year for that coverage. Her practice, which represented most of her net worth, carried zero business interruption coverage.

That's the pattern we see across dentists, attorneys, and service-based business owners: too much insurance in low-risk corners, not enough where one event could erase years of savings. The fix isn't a checklist. It's a math problem: your maximum realistic loss minus what your cash reserves or net worth could already absorb. That gap is what you insure.

We walk through the exact numbers for personal and business coverage, including umbrella, disability, and business interruption sizing, in the full post.

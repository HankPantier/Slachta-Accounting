## LinkedIn

An attorney who donates 20 hours drafting bylaws for a nonprofit, at her normal $400 hourly rate, still gets a $0 deduction for that time. The IRS has been clear on this for years: your hours are worth nothing on Schedule A, no matter your billing rate as a dentist, attorney, or architect.

What is deductible: mileage at 14 cents per mile (a rate unchanged since 1997), required uniforms, supplies bought specifically for the work, and travel away from home when the trip is genuinely for the organization, not a vacation with a meeting attached.

For business owners who sit on hospital foundation boards, bar committees, or school boards, this comes up more than people expect. Cross $250 in expenses with one organization and you need a written acknowledgment, not a verbal thank-you at the gala.

## LinkedIn

A dentist in Hudson, Wisconsin went three years without a real day off. The result: two missed insurance fee-schedule updates that cost him roughly $40,000 in underbilled claims.

That's decision fatigue showing up on a P&L, not in a mood. Tired brains default to the easiest choice, not the best one, which means deferred hiring, aging receivables, and rate increases that never happen. For dentists, attorneys, architects, and service-based business owners, that pattern compounds fast: a missed fee schedule in month one becomes a missed fee schedule in month eleven without a standing quarterly review.

Rest isn't the reward for a stable business. It's part of what keeps judgment sharp enough to run one. Read how a recurring financial review builds that buffer: https://www.slachtacpa.com/resources/burnout-isnt-a-business-strategy-self-care-should-be-part-of

## Practical guidance for business owners, dentists, and entrepreneurs

Most accounting firm websites bury useful information behind a blog feed nobody opens twice. This library works differently. Every guide, tool, and article here answers a question we hear repeatedly in meetings with dentists, service-based business owners, and entrepreneurs: how do I manage cash flow, evaluate a retirement plan, build a budget that holds up, or track the KPIs that predict trouble before it shows up on a bank statement?

Slachta Accounting & Consulting Services LLC built this collection from client conversations dating back to 2005, when the firm opened its doors in Stillwater, Minnesota. You will not find advice recycled from a wire service. You will find the same guidance our CPAs give clients across Minnesota, Wisconsin, and nationwide, organized so you can find what applies to your situation today.

## Guides for dentists and service-based businesses

Generic small business advice tends to miss the details that matter inside a dental practice or a service-based shop. These sections go deeper.

### Dentists
icon: Stethoscope

Practice overhead, associate compensation, equipment financing, and retirement plan design all come with rules specific to dentistry. Our dentist-focused resources cover the numbers that matter chairside and at the bank. [See dentist resources](/industries/dentists)

### Service-based businesses
icon: Briefcase

Labor is usually the largest line item, and utilization rates drive profit more than top-line revenue does. These guides focus on pricing, staffing costs, and the cash flow patterns typical of service firms. [See service business resources](/industries/service-based-businesses)

### Entrepreneurs and established small businesses
icon: Lightbulb

Three years in or thirty, the questions shift over time: entity structure and cash reserves early on, succession and exit planning later. Resources here track both stages. [See entrepreneur resources](/industries/entrepreneurs-established-small-businesses)

- An expense tracker designed to flag categories creeping out of line

Pull one, fill it in, and bring the results to your next conversation with your CPA or your financial advisor.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Resources library

**Q: What kind of content is in the resources library?**
A: The library includes eBooks, Advantage Magazine issues, QuickReads news updates, and articles organized by tax, payroll, and outsourced accounting topics. Content is built around real client questions on cash flow, budgeting, KPIs, and retirement planning rather than generic industry news.

**Q: Is there content specific to dentists or service-based businesses?**
A: Yes. The library includes dedicated sections for dentists, service-based businesses, and entrepreneurs, covering issues like practice overhead, associate compensation, staffing costs, and utilization rates that generic small business advice usually overlooks.

**Q: Do I need to be a Slachta client to use these resources?**
A: No. The library is open to anyone researching tax, payroll, or accounting questions. Many visitors use it to evaluate the firm before reaching out, though the tools and guides are useful on their own.

**Q: How often is new content added to the library?**
A: QuickReads content is updated regularly to reflect current tax law changes, economic data, and industry trends, while eBooks and Advantage Magazine issues are added seasonally as new planning topics become relevant.

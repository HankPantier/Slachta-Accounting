## What outsourced accounting and payroll support looks like for growing businesses

For most owners, the pain point isn't ambition. It's expense management and cash flow, the two items that top nearly every small-business-worry survey. Outsourced accounting puts trained eyes on the books every month, not just at tax time, which is the difference between reacting to a cash crunch and seeing it coming three weeks out.

- Monthly bookkeeping and reconciliation, so you know your numbers by the 10th, not the 30th
- Payroll processing for W-2 staff, 1099 contractors, and multi-location teams, handled on schedule every pay period
- Budgeting built around your actual cash cycle instead of a generic template
- KPI tracking tied to the metrics that move your specific business, like production per provider or billable utilization
- Cash flow forecasting that flags a shortfall before it becomes a crisis

## Specialized guidance for dentists and service-based businesses

Generic bookkeeping misses the details that matter in dentistry and professional services. Two examples show why niche knowledge changes the outcome.

### Dentists
icon: Stethoscope

Dental practices run on production numbers, hygiene schedules, and payroll that spans associates, hygienists, and front-desk staff on different pay structures. We track overhead ratios and production-per-provider, coordinate payroll around bonus and profit-sharing structures, and flag the cash flow dips that come with slow insurance reimbursements.

### Service-based businesses
icon: Briefcase

Insurance agencies, architecture firms, and attorneys share a pattern: revenue tied to billable hours or commission cycles, and expenses that spike before income catches up. We build budgets around that timing instead of against it, so payroll and vendor bills never outrun collections.

## Tax planning and preparation that supports long-term business decisions

Filing a return in March tells you what already happened. Real tax planning happens in July, September, and November, while there's still time to change the outcome. Business tax services here start with a mid-year projection, so you know your estimated liability before the fourth quarter, not after.

From there, we look at retirement plan options, SEP-IRA, SIMPLE, or a 401(k) with profit sharing, and evaluate which structure reduces the current tax bill while building the owner's retirement savings. We also coordinate directly with your financial advisor, so tax strategy and investment strategy pull in the same direction instead of working against each other. For an entrepreneur weighing a new hire, a second location, or an equipment purchase, that kind of planning turns a tax deadline into an actual decision point.

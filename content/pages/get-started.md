## Take the first step toward clearer financials

Booking a consultation shouldn't feel like a big commitment, and at Slachta Accounting & Consulting Services, it isn't one. It's a conversation. You tell us where your business or practice stands today, what's keeping you up at night, whether that's cash flow, payroll headaches, or a tax bill that caught you off guard, and we listen.

Business owners, dentists, attorneys, architects, and specialty healthcare providers reach out to us at different stages: some are five years into a practice and outgrowing their current bookkeeping setup, others are just starting out and want a CPA involved from day one. Either way, there's no sales pitch waiting for you. Just a straightforward look at where you are and whether our outsourced accounting, payroll, and tax services are the right fit.

<!-- block: content-split | variant: image-right | image: consultation-phone-call.jpg | alt: "Business owner having a friendly phone consultation with an accountant" | query: "professional phone consultation office" -->
## What to expect from your first conversation

Your first conversation with us runs 30 to 45 minutes, by phone, video, or in person at our Stillwater, Minnesota office, whichever works better for your schedule. There's no script we're reading from. We ask about your business structure, your current bookkeeping or payroll setup, and what's driving you to look for a CPA firm right now.

For established businesses, that often means digging into cash flow patterns, the KPIs you're tracking (or should be), and whether your retirement plan still fits your workforce and your goals. For newer entrepreneurs, the conversation leans toward setting up systems correctly the first time: chart of accounts, payroll structure, and a tax plan that doesn't leave money on the table.

We'll also ask who else is on your team, your financial advisor, attorney, or insurance agent, because good financial planning works best when your advisors are talking to each other, not working in silos. By the end of the call, you'll know whether we're a fit, and what working together would actually look like.

## How we prepare to make the most of your time

We've been doing this since 2005, first in Stillwater, Minnesota, and that history means we've learned how to make a first meeting productive instead of a formality. Coming prepared with a few basics helps us give you useful answers on the spot rather than a list of follow-up questions.

Here's what's helpful to have on hand, though nothing here is required to book:

- Your most recent business tax return, if you have one
- A recent profit and loss statement or bank statement
- Current payroll provider information, if applicable
- Details on any existing retirement plan
- A short list of the two or three financial questions bothering you most

If you don't have all of this ready, come anyway. We'd rather have the conversation now and gather documents later than have you delay reaching out.

## Serving clients across Minnesota, Wisconsin, and beyond

Our roots are in Stillwater, Minnesota, and we still work with plenty of business owners and dentists across Minnesota and Wisconsin who prefer meeting face to face. But most of our client relationships today run just as well over video or phone, and we work with clients nationwide who never set foot in our office.

Wherever you're located, the process for a first consultation looks the same: a real conversation about your numbers, not a generic intake form. Distance doesn't change how closely we look at your cash flow, your KPIs, or your tax position.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Schedule your consultation

**Q: How long does the first consultation take?**
A: Most initial consultations run 30 to 45 minutes, whether by phone, video, or in person at our Stillwater, Minnesota office. We use that time to understand your business, current financial setup, and the specific challenges driving you to look for CPA support right now.

**Q: Do I need to prepare anything before scheduling?**
A: No preparation is required, though bringing a recent tax return, profit and loss statement, or payroll details helps us give more specific answers during the call. If you don't have documents ready, book anyway; we'd rather talk now and follow up later.

**Q: Is the consultation available if I'm outside Minnesota or Wisconsin?**
A: Yes. While the firm is based in Stillwater, Minnesota and serves many clients across Minnesota and Wisconsin, consultations are available by phone or video for business owners anywhere in the country.

**Q: What types of businesses does Slachta CPA typically work with?**
A: The firm focuses on dentists, service-based businesses, established entrepreneurs, attorneys, architects, and specialty healthcare providers. If your business fits one of these categories, the consultation is built to address the financial questions specific to your industry.

**Q: Is there a cost or obligation to schedule a consultation?**
A: No. The initial consultation is a no-cost conversation to determine fit, discuss your current financial picture, and outline how outsourced accounting, payroll, or tax services might help. There's no obligation to move forward afterward.

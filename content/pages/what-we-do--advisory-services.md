## Strategic guidance beyond tax prep and bookkeeping

Tax returns tell you what happened. Advisory work at Slachta Accounting & Consulting Services helps you decide what happens next. Founded in Stillwater, Minnesota in 2005, our firm built its advisory practice around a simple observation: business owners who see their numbers once a year make decisions with old information.

We meet with clients throughout the year, not just at filing season, to talk through pricing changes, staffing plans, and equipment purchases before they happen. That timing matters. A conversation in March about a hiring decision is worth more than a tax return in April explaining why the payroll number came in high.

Advisory services sit alongside outsourced accounting and payroll work, but the goal here is different: fewer surprises, better-informed decisions, and a clearer read on where the business stands right now.

## Cash flow management and budgeting support

Cash flow problems rarely show up as a missing number on a report. They show up as a payroll run you're not sure you can cover, or a vendor invoice that lands the same week as a tax payment. Advisory engagements at Slachta focus on catching that mismatch before it becomes a crisis.

We build rolling cash forecasts with clients so seasonal dips, slow-paying customers, and large one-time expenses show up on the calendar weeks in advance, not the day they're due. Budgets get built the same way: grounded in actual historical spending by category, then revisited quarterly as the business changes.

For an owner already juggling a practice, a client roster, or a project schedule, this is the piece that turns anxiety about the bank balance into a plan with real numbers attached.

<!-- block: checklist-section | variant: with-image-left | image: kpi-dashboard-review.jpg | alt: "Accountant pointing to KPI dashboard on a laptop screen with client" | query: "accountant reviewing performance dashboard client" -->
## KPI tracking and performance benchmarking

Revenue growth alone doesn't tell you whether a business is healthy. Overhead percentage, labor cost as a share of revenue, days in accounts receivable, and client retention often say more about where things stand than the top line does.

We help clients pick the three to five metrics that actually match their business, then build simple reporting so those numbers show up monthly instead of getting reconstructed at year-end. For a dental practice, that might mean production per hour and overhead ratio. For a service-based business, it's often gross margin by service line and staff utilization.

- Track overhead ratio and labor cost percentage month over month
- Monitor accounts receivable aging and collection speed
- Measure margin by service line, product, or client type
- Compare current performance against your own prior-year benchmarks, not generic industry averages

## Retirement plan evaluation and financial advisor collaboration

Retirement plan decisions, whether that's a SEP IRA, a 401(k) with profit sharing, or a cash balance plan for a dental practice, touch both business tax strategy and personal financial planning. Slachta reviews plan design and contribution limits from the tax side, then works directly with the client's existing financial advisor to make sure the plan and the investment strategy line up.

We're not here to replace that advisor or manage investments. Our role is the accounting and tax lens: what a given plan structure costs the business, what it saves in taxes, and how contribution levels interact with cash flow the rest of the year. That coordination keeps the advisor, the CPA, and the owner working from the same numbers instead of three different assumptions.

For owners without a financial advisor yet, we can point toward that conversation. For those who already have one, we join the call.

## Advisory support tailored to dentists and service-based businesses

Advisory work looks different depending on what you're running. Two niches in particular, dentists and service-based businesses, bring recurring questions that generic advice doesn't answer.

### Dental practices
icon: Stethoscope

Overhead ratio, hygiene production per hour, and staff-to-chair ratios drive profitability in a dental practice more than most owners realize until someone points it out. We benchmark these numbers against the practice's own history and help evaluate decisions like adding an associate or expanding hours.

### Service-based businesses
icon: Wrench

Pricing and margin by service line get murky fast when a business bills hourly, by project, and by retainer at the same time. We help owners see which offerings actually make money, which ones are subsidizing the others, and where prices need to move.

## How advisory pairs with outsourced accounting and payroll

Advisory conversations work best with current numbers behind them, which is why most advisory clients also use our [outsourced accounting](/what-we-do/outsourced-accounting) or [payroll services](/services/payroll-services). When the same firm handles the bookkeeping, the payroll runs, and the strategy conversation, there's no lag between a decision and the data needed to make it.

Clients who only need advisory input, without handing off the accounting itself, work with us that way too. Either arrangement gives you a direct line to a CPA who already knows your numbers, rather than someone meeting your business for the first time each quarter.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Advisory services

**Q: What is included in accounting advisory services for small businesses?**
A: Advisory engagements typically cover cash flow forecasting, budgeting, KPI tracking, and retirement plan evaluation coordinated with your financial advisor. At Slachta, the scope is set with each client based on what decisions they're facing, not a fixed package.

**Q: Do I need outsourced accounting or payroll to get advisory services?**
A: No. Advisory services can stand on their own, though most clients who already use Slachta's outsourced accounting or payroll services find the strategy conversations move faster because current numbers are already in hand.

**Q: Will Slachta replace my financial advisor for retirement planning?**
A: No. Slachta evaluates retirement plan design and tax impact and works directly with your existing financial advisor on investment strategy. The firm does not manage investments or replace outside advisor relationships.

**Q: What KPIs should a small business track?**
A: It depends on the business. A dental practice often watches overhead ratio and production per hour, while a service-based business tracks margin by service line and utilization. Slachta helps identify the three to five metrics that fit each client's operation.

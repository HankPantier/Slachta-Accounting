## Getting to know Kendra Quist

Kendra Quist works with the business owners who need more than a once-a-year tax return; they need someone tracking the numbers between filings. As an outsourced accounting specialist at Slachta Accounting & Consulting Services LLC in Stillwater, Minnesota, she spends her days inside the books of dental practices, service-based businesses, and established small companies across Minnesota and Wisconsin.

Clients usually come to her with the same problem: the bookkeeping is behind, payroll feels like guesswork, and nobody has had time to build a real budget. Kendra sorts through the mess, sets up a system that fits how the business actually runs, and stays involved so it keeps working after the first few months. Owners describe her as direct, responsive, and quick to explain a financial statement without the jargon.

<!-- block: content-split | variant: image-right | image: kendra-quist-dental-client-review.jpg | alt: "Accountant reviewing financial statements with a dental practice owner" | query: "accountant reviewing documents dental office" -->
## How Kendra supports dentists and service-based businesses

Dental practices and service-based businesses run on tight margins and heavy overhead: supplies, staffing, equipment, and the steady cost of keeping the doors open. Kendra handles the outsourced bookkeeping and payroll work that lets owners stop reconciling accounts at 9 p.m. and start reading their numbers instead.

She builds monthly close processes for dental practices that flag supply costs and production trends early, and she sets up payroll cycles for service-based businesses juggling hourly staff, contractors, and overtime rules. Because she works across both niches regularly, she recognizes patterns fast: a dentist's collections slipping against production, or a contractor's payroll creeping ahead of revenue. That pattern recognition is what turns a monthly bookkeeping engagement into an early warning system.

<!-- block: checklist-section | variant: with-image | image: kendra-quist-cash-flow-planning.jpg | alt: "Accountant walking a small business owner through a cash flow report" | query: "small business owner reviewing financial report" -->
## Her approach to payroll, tax planning, and cash flow guidance

Kendra treats payroll and tax planning as ongoing conversations, not year-end scrambles. Entrepreneurs and established small business owners she works with typically need help in the same few areas, and she builds her engagements around them:

- Setting up payroll processing that accounts for hourly staff, contractors, and benefits without errors at each pay cycle
- Building expense management habits that catch overspending before it shows up on a quarterly report
- Creating budgets tied to actual cash flow instead of last year's guesswork
- Tracking key performance indicators specific to the business, from production numbers at a dental practice to billable hours at a service firm
- Coordinating quarterly tax planning so estimated payments and deductions are handled before December, not after

She also works alongside clients' financial advisors when personal financial planning questions come up, keeping business tax strategy and personal goals pointed in the same direction.

## Professional background and credentials

Kendra built her career in outsourced accounting, payroll, and small business tax work before joining Slachta Accounting & Consulting Services LLC in Stillwater, Minnesota. Her background centers on the day-to-day mechanics that keep a small business solvent: monthly close, payroll compliance, and the kind of budgeting that holds up when revenue is uneven.

At the firm, she works primarily with dentists, service-based businesses, and entrepreneurs across Minnesota and Wisconsin, applying accounting standards to the specific rhythms of each industry rather than a generic template. That focus is why dental practices and service firms tend to stay with her year after year: she already knows the questions they're going to ask before they ask them.

## Outside the office

When she's not closing out a client's books, Kendra is usually somewhere around Stillwater, walking the bluffs above the St. Croix River or catching up with neighbors at a local shop downtown. She's involved in the kind of small-town routines that make Stillwater feel like Stillwater: supporting local businesses, showing up for community events, and staying connected to the same Main Street the firm has called home since 2005.

That local footing shapes how she works with clients, too. She's not managing accounts from a call center. She's a familiar face who understands what it means to run a business in this part of Minnesota and Wisconsin.

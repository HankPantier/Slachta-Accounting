## Outsourced accounting that runs like an in-house team

When you hand your books to Slachta, you get a bookkeeper, a controller, and a second set of eyes that catches problems before they cost you money. We work inside a secure online platform, so you can log in and see your cash position, payables, and receivables on the day you need the answer, not three weeks after month-end close.

Most outsourced accounting arrangements feel like sending your data into a black box. This one doesn't. You get monthly financials, a direct line to the person who prepared them, and a workflow built around your schedule rather than a vendor's. Business owners in Stillwater, Minnesota, and across Wisconsin use this setup to replace an internal accounting hire or to add capacity a small internal team doesn't have time for.

Services bundle into a fixed monthly fee, so a slow November doesn't turn into a surprise invoice in December. See the full [outsourced accounting](/what-we-do/outsourced-accounting) lineup for package details.

## Payroll handled accurately and on time, every time

Missed a payroll tax deadline once and you know why owners stop doing this in-house. Slachta's [payroll services](/services/payroll-services) cover the full cycle so nothing falls through:

- Payroll processing and direct deposit on your schedule, weekly, biweekly, or monthly
- Federal and state payroll tax filings, calculated and submitted on time
- New hire reporting and quarterly compliance filings
- Year-end W-2 and 1099 preparation
- Direct support when an employee has a question about a paycheck

Dental practices, insurance agencies, law firms, architecture firms, and specialty healthcare services rarely have the staff hours to own this correctly. Outsourcing payroll to a firm that also handles your books means one team, one set of numbers, and no gap between what payroll reports and what your financials show.

## Business tax services that plan ahead, not just file

A tax return is a record of decisions you already made. The return itself doesn't save you anything; the planning that happens beforehand does. Slachta's business tax work runs year-round: quarterly check-ins on estimated payments, entity structure reviews as your business grows, and a look at upcoming purchases or hires before they hit your tax bill instead of after.

Entrepreneurs and established small business owners come to us after a few years of filing with someone who only calls in March. That model works fine until the business changes, adds a partner, buys equipment, or crosses into a new tax bracket, and the old approach stops catching what matters. Our [tax planning and preparation](/what-we-do/tax-plus) work is built to catch it, because the plan gets revisited before decisions get made, not after.

Filing still happens, accurately and on deadline. It's just not the whole job.

## Cash flow, budgeting, and KPI guidance that support decisions

Expense management and cash flow keep small business owners up at night more than almost anything else. Numbers alone don't fix that; context does. Slachta builds the reports that turn raw transactions into decisions you can actually make.

### Cash Flow Forecasting
icon: ChartLine

We project cash position weeks and months out, so you can time a hire, a purchase, or a slow season without guessing.

### Budgeting Support
icon: Calculator

Annual budgets get built against your actual historical numbers, then reviewed against real results throughout the year, not filed away in January.

### KPI Dashboards
icon: ChartBar

We identify the two or three metrics that actually move your business, whether that's production per chair, billable hours, or gross margin by service line, and track them on a schedule you set.

## Retirement plan and financial advisor collaboration

Retirement plan decisions touch payroll, tax planning, and personal financial goals all at once, which is exactly why they get handled poorly when only one advisor is in the room. Slachta sits on the accounting and tax side of that conversation and works directly with your financial advisor to evaluate plan options: SEP-IRA, SIMPLE IRA, 401(k), or a defined benefit plan for a dentist a few years from selling a practice.

The same coordination applies to personal financial planning. Your business decisions affect your personal tax picture, and your advisor needs accurate numbers to plan around. We provide those numbers directly to your advisor rather than making you relay them, so the two sides of your financial life stay in sync instead of working from different assumptions.

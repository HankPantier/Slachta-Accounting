## Make a payment

Paying your bill shouldn't require a phone call or a hunt through old emails. Slachta Accounting & Consulting Services keeps it simple: open the secure client payment portal, enter your invoice details, and confirm. Most card payments post within one business day, and you'll get an email receipt the moment the transaction clears. Returning clients can save a payment method for faster checkout next time. New here? You can pay a one-time invoice without creating an account at all.

<!-- block: checklist-section | variant: with-image | image: online-payment-methods.jpg | alt: "Person entering credit card details on a laptop for an online payment" | query: "person paying bill online laptop" -->
## Accepted payment methods

We accept the payment methods most business owners already use day to day:

- Credit and debit card (Visa, Mastercard, Discover, American Express)
- ACH bank transfer directly from your checking or savings account
- Paper check mailed to our Stillwater, Minnesota office

Card payments typically post within one business day. ACH transfers usually take two to three business days to clear, since they route through the banking network rather than a card processor. A small processing fee applies to card payments, and the portal discloses the exact amount before you confirm. ACH transfers and mailed checks carry no added fee. Choose whichever method fits your cash flow.

<!-- block: checklist-section | variant: with-image-left | image: invoice-details-checklist.jpg | alt: "Business owner reviewing an invoice and account details before paying" | query: "person reviewing invoice paperwork desk" -->
## What to have ready before you pay

Before you head to the portal, gather a few details so your payment matches the right account on the first try:

- Your invoice number, printed in the top corner of the bill we sent
- The billing name on your account, individual or business
- The exact amount due, including any partial payment you're making
- Your card number, or your bank routing and account number for ACH

Having these on hand removes the guesswork and prevents delays caused by a mismatched account name or a transposed digit. If anything on your invoice looks unfamiliar, reach out before you pay rather than after. We would rather clarify a line item up front than process a refund later.

<!-- block: content-split | variant: image-left | image: billing-question-call.jpg | alt: "Accountant on a phone call helping a client with a billing question" | query: "office phone call client support" -->
## Questions about your invoice or bill

Billing questions come up, especially when your invoice combines more than one engagement, like payroll processing and a quarterly tax review on the same statement. If a charge doesn't match what you expected, or you need to spread a larger balance across two or three payments, [contact our office directly](/contact) rather than guessing at the portal. We check every discrepancy against your engagement letter and prior invoices before we respond, so you get a real answer instead of a form-letter apology. Payment plan requests are handled case by case, based on the size of the invoice and your account history with us.

<!-- block: content-prose -->
## Security and privacy of your payment

Your payment information is encrypted the moment you enter it, and our office never sees or stores your full card or bank account number. The processor behind our payment portal meets PCI-DSS standards, the same benchmark banks and major retailers use to handle card transactions. We use your billing details only to apply your payment to the correct account and send a receipt, nothing more. If you'd rather skip entering payment details online, a check mailed to our Stillwater office works just as well.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Payment

**Q: What payment methods does Slachta Accounting accept?**
A: We accept credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Discover, American Express), ACH bank transfers, and paper checks mailed to our Stillwater, Minnesota office. Card payments run through our secure online portal; checks can be mailed directly to the firm.

**Q: How long does it take for my payment to process?**
A: Card payments made through the portal typically post within one business day. ACH bank transfers usually take two to three business days to clear because they route through the banking network rather than a card processor.

**Q: Is there a fee for paying my invoice by credit card?**
A: A small processing fee applies to card payments, and the portal discloses the exact amount before you confirm. ACH bank transfers and mailed checks carry no added processing fee.

**Q: Who do I contact if I have a question about my invoice?**
A: Contact our billing team directly rather than guessing through the portal. We review discrepancies against your engagement letter and prior invoices, and can also set up a payment plan if a balance needs to be split across multiple payments.

## Let's talk about your business

Slachta Accounting & Consulting Services has worked out of Stillwater, Minnesota since 2005. Dentists, attorneys, architects, insurance agencies, and service-based business owners across Minnesota and Wisconsin call on us when cash flow gets tight or a budgeting decision needs a second opinion. Clients outside those two states work with us as well, since most of what we do happens by phone, video, and secure file sharing. Tell us what's going on in your business, and we'll tell you plainly whether we're the right fit to help.

<!-- block: checklist-section | variant: with-image | image: accountant-answering-phone-office.jpg | alt: "Accountant at a desk answering a phone call in a small office" | query: "accountant office phone call professional" -->
## Ways to reach our team

You don't need to navigate a phone tree or wait a week for a reply. Call our Stillwater office during business hours and you'll reach a real person who can point you in the right direction. Email works just as well for most questions, and messages usually get a response within one business day. If you'd rather lay out your situation in writing first, the contact form below goes straight to one of our accountants, not a general inbox.

- Phone: speak directly with our Stillwater team during business hours
- Email: send details about your business and expect a reply within one business day
- Contact form: describe what you need below and we'll follow up personally, not with an automated response

<!-- block: content-split | variant: image-left | image: stillwater-minnesota-office-exterior.jpg | alt: "Small-town office building exterior in downtown Stillwater Minnesota" | query: "downtown Stillwater Minnesota office building" -->
## Visit our Stillwater office

Prefer to talk face-to-face? Our office sits in downtown Stillwater, Minnesota, an easy drive from the Twin Cities and from western Wisconsin. Street parking and a small lot behind the building make short visits simple. We keep regular weekday hours and meet by appointment, so a quick call or email ahead of time makes sure someone's free when you arrive. If the drive doesn't work for your schedule, a video call covers the same ground.

## What to expect when you contact us

### Initial conversation
We start with a short call to learn what's happening in your business right now, whether that's a cash flow crunch, a payroll headache, or a tax deadline closing in.

### Discovery of your needs
Next we ask about your entity structure, your current bookkeeping setup, and how you're tracking KPIs, so we know exactly where the gaps are before recommending anything.

### Matching you with the right service
Based on that conversation, we recommend outsourced accounting, payroll, business tax services, or some combination, and walk through what working together would actually look like month to month.

### Engagement and onboarding
Once you decide to move forward, we set up your files, system access, and reporting schedule so nothing slips through the cracks in the first few months.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Contact us

**Q: What industries does Slachta Accounting & Consulting Services work with?**
A: The firm concentrates on dentists, attorneys, architects, insurance agencies, specialty healthcare services, and service-based business owners. Most clients are in Minnesota and Wisconsin, though the firm serves businesses nationwide through phone, video, and secure file sharing.

**Q: Do you work with clients outside Minnesota and Wisconsin?**
A: Yes. While the firm is based in Stillwater, Minnesota and has a strong concentration of clients in Minnesota and Wisconsin, outsourced accounting, payroll, and business tax services are delivered nationwide using video calls and secure online tools.

**Q: How quickly will I hear back after contacting the firm?**
A: Email and contact form submissions typically get a reply within one business day. Phone calls made during business hours reach a team member directly rather than an automated system.

**Q: How does billing work once I become a client?**
A: Billing depends on the services you choose, whether that's outsourced accounting, payroll, or business tax services. Your accountant will walk through pricing and payment terms during onboarding, before any work begins.

**Q: Is the initial consultation free?**
A: Yes. The first conversation is a no-cost discovery call to understand your business, whether you're dealing with cash flow issues, payroll setup, or an upcoming tax deadline, before we recommend any service.

## Financial guidance for every stage of business ownership

Starting a business and running one for fifteen years call for different kinds of financial support. Slachta Accounting & Consulting Services LLC has worked with entrepreneurs and established business owners since 2005, based in Stillwater, Minnesota. The questions change as a company grows. A founder in year one needs help setting up bookkeeping and choosing an entity structure. A founder in year twelve needs a second opinion on retirement plan design, a clearer read on margins, and a CPA who already knows the numbers.

Government checklists and generic startup guides answer the first set of questions well. They stop short of the second. Our team builds ongoing relationships with business owners, dentists, attorneys, architects, and specialty healthcare practices across Minnesota, Wisconsin, and clients nationwide, so the guidance keeps pace with the business instead of resetting every time a new problem shows up.

## Solving the cash flow and expense challenges small businesses face

Most small businesses don't fail because revenue disappears. They fail because cash runs out while the books still say things look fine. Expense creep, slow-paying customers, and seasonal swings can erode a healthy-looking business month by month, and by the time it shows up on a year-end statement, the window to fix it has closed.

We watch the numbers between statements, not just at tax time. That means:

- Monthly cash flow forecasting so surprises show up weeks early instead of after the fact
- Expense categorization that flags creeping costs before they harden into fixed costs
- Budget-to-actual reviews tied to real bank activity, not last year's guesswork
- Early flags on receivables aging so slow-paying clients don't quietly drain your cash

## Retirement plan evaluation and personal financial planning collaboration

Business owners often have a financial advisor for personal investments and a payroll provider handling the retirement plan, and the two rarely talk. We sit at the table with both. When it's time to evaluate a SIMPLE IRA against a 401(k), or decide whether a safe harbor provision still fits the business, we bring the tax and cash flow picture your advisor needs to make that call.

The same collaboration extends to personal financial planning. A business owner's compensation, distributions, and retirement contributions affect personal tax exposure, and the reverse is also true. Rather than treating those as separate conversations, we coordinate directly with your financial advisor so the business and personal sides of your finances point in the same direction.

## Tracking the KPIs that actually drive business decisions

### Margin and profitability
icon: ChartLine

Gross margin by service line or product tells you which parts of the business are actually making money, not just which parts are busiest.

### Labor and overhead ratios
icon: Users

Labor cost as a percentage of revenue catches staffing decisions that quietly outpace growth, before payroll becomes the largest line item on the P&L.

### Cash conversion cycle
icon: Clock

How long cash sits in receivables and inventory before it's usable again shapes decisions on financing, hiring, and expansion timing.

## Tax strategy for business owners in Minnesota, Wisconsin and beyond

Slachta Accounting & Consulting Services LLC has served business clients from Stillwater, Minnesota since 2005, with a client base concentrated in Minnesota and Wisconsin and reaching business owners nationwide.

## Accounting support built around your business, not a template

Slachta Accounting & Consulting Services has worked out of Stillwater, Minnesota, since 2005. Two decades in, the approach hasn't changed: understand how a business actually runs, then build accounting support around it, not the other way around. Business owners, dentists, insurance agencies, attorneys, architects, and specialty healthcare practices make up the client base, and each one needs something a little different from the next.

That's why this page works as a hub rather than a single service description. Below, you'll find outsourced accounting and bookkeeping, payroll processing, business tax planning and preparation, and advisory work covering cash flow, budgeting, and retirement plan evaluation. The firm serves clients nationwide, with the strongest concentration of relationships in Minnesota and Wisconsin. Whichever service you start with, the goal is the same: fewer surprises at tax time and a clearer read on where the business stands.

<!-- block: content-split | variant: image-right | image: outsourced-bookkeeping-review.jpg | alt: "Accountant reconciling financial statements on a laptop for a small business client" | query: "accountant reviewing financial statements laptop" -->
## Outsourced accounting and bookkeeping that keeps your books audit-ready

Books that are three weeks behind don't help anyone make a decision. [Outsourced accounting](/what-we-do/outsourced-accounting) through Slachta means transactions get categorized, accounts get reconciled, and financial statements go out on a schedule you can actually plan around, not whenever someone finds time.

For small businesses juggling expense management and cash flow, that regularity matters more than any software feature. You see what's coming in, what's going out, and where the gaps are before they become a problem. Monthly financial statements, balance sheet reconciliations, and general ledger maintenance are handled by the same team every month, so nothing gets lost between staff turnover or software switches.

This isn't data entry with a fancy name. It's the foundation that makes tax planning, payroll, and advisory conversations possible later in the year. Clients ranging from single-location dental practices to multi-partner law firms rely on this service to keep their books ready for a lender, an auditor, or a buyer, whenever that day comes.

<!-- block: content-split | variant: image-left | image: payroll-processing-small-business.jpg | alt: "Payroll specialist processing employee paychecks on a computer" | query: "payroll processing office computer" -->
## Payroll processing that keeps your team paid accurately and on time

Payroll has a way of turning into a second job. Between withholding calculations, quarterly filings, and changing state requirements, a lot of small business owners and dental practice managers end up doing payroll instead of running the business.

[Payroll services](/services/payroll-services) from Slachta handle the calculations, the tax deposits, the filings, and the direct deposits, on a schedule your employees can count on. Dental practices deal with a mix of hourly hygienists, salaried associates, and bonus structures that off-the-shelf payroll software wasn't built to handle cleanly. We set it up once and run it correctly every pay period after that.

If you're comparing payroll processing to a do-it-yourself app, the real cost isn't the subscription fee. It's the hours spent fixing it when something's wrong, plus the penalty notice that shows up three months later.

## Business tax services that plan ahead instead of just filing returns

A tax return filed in March reflects decisions made the previous January through December. By the time it's being prepared, most of the opportunity to change the outcome is already gone.

Slachta's [business tax services](/what-we-do/tax-plus) start earlier than that. Entrepreneurs and established small businesses meet with us during the year, not just at filing season, to look at entity structure, estimated payments, and the timing of major purchases or income. That conversation separates tax planning from tax preparation, and it's why clients come back year after year instead of shopping for the lowest filing fee.

Business tax preparation itself covers federal and state returns, quarterly estimates, and the documentation to back up every deduction claimed. The planning work, projecting liability before year-end, evaluating retirement plan contributions, deciding whether an S-corp election still makes sense, is where the real savings usually show up. If your current preparer only calls in March, that's a sign it might be time for something different.

## Specialized accounting for dentists and service-based businesses

Generic accounting advice runs out fast once you're past basic bookkeeping. A dental practice manages overhead differently than a law firm, and a growing service business has different cash flow patterns than an established one. Here's where that specialization shows up.

### Dentists
icon: Stethoscope

[Dental practices](/industries/dentists) carry equipment financing, associate compensation, and hygiene department margins that general bookkeeping doesn't track well. We handle the accounting layer so practice owners can focus on patient care and staffing decisions instead of spreadsheets.

### Service-Based Businesses
icon: Briefcase

[Insurance agencies, consultants, and other service firms](/industries/service-based-businesses) often bill on retainers, commissions, or project fees, which need to be tracked differently than product sales. We set up the reporting to match how the revenue actually comes in.

### Attorneys and Architects
icon: Scale

Trust accounting, project-based billing, and partner distributions each carry their own compliance requirements. Our work with [law firms](/industries/law) and [architecture practices](/industries/architects) covers the accounting detail those structures demand.

### Entrepreneurs and Established Small Businesses
icon: TrendingUp

Early-stage decisions about entity structure and bookkeeping systems affect a business for years. We work with [owners at both ends](/industries/entrepreneurs-established-small-businesses), the startup phase and the established, multi-employee stage, adjusting the level of support as the business grows.

## Financial guidance for cash flow, budgeting, and retirement planning decisions

Compliance work, tax returns, payroll, bookkeeping, keeps a business legal and organized. It doesn't tell you whether the business is actually healthy. That's a separate conversation, and Slachta has it with clients throughout the year, not just when something's gone wrong.

This [advisory work](/what-we-do/advisory-services) includes:

- Evaluating retirement plan options and contribution strategies for owners and staff
- Coordinating with your financial advisor on personal financial planning that intersects with the business
- Building cash flow projections that flag shortfalls before they hit
- Setting budgets tied to actual historical performance, not guesswork
- Tracking the KPIs that matter for your specific business, not a generic dashboard

None of this replaces your financial advisor. It gives them, and you, better numbers to work from.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Services

**Q: What is included in outsourced accounting services?**
A: Outsourced accounting typically covers bookkeeping, bank and credit card reconciliations, monthly financial statement preparation, and general ledger maintenance. At Slachta, it also includes cash flow visibility and expense tracking so business owners can make decisions without waiting for year-end numbers.

**Q: Does Slachta offer payroll services for dental practices?**
A: Yes. Slachta processes payroll for dental practices with mixed staffing structures, including hourly hygienists, salaried associates, and bonus arrangements, handling tax deposits, filings, and direct deposits on a consistent schedule.

**Q: How is business tax planning different from tax preparation?**
A: Tax preparation files the return for a year that's already happened. Tax planning happens throughout the year and looks at entity structure, estimated payments, and retirement contributions before deadlines lock in the outcome, which is the approach Slachta takes with each of its niche clients.

**Q: Does Slachta work with businesses outside Minnesota and Wisconsin?**
A: Yes. Slachta serves clients nationwide, though the firm's deepest concentration of relationships is in Minnesota and Wisconsin, where it has operated since founding in Stillwater, Minnesota, in 2005.

**Q: What industries does Slachta specialize in?**
A: Slachta focuses on dentists, insurance agencies, architects, law firms, and specialty healthcare services, tailoring bookkeeping, payroll, and tax work to how each of those business types actually operates.

**Q: Does Slachta work with specialty healthcare practices?**
A: Yes. Specialty healthcare practices have staffing structures, billing cycles, and overhead patterns that differ from general business accounting. Slachta's accounting and payroll work is set up to reflect how those practices actually run.

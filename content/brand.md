# About Slachta Accounting & Consulting Services LLC

## Identity

Slachta Accounting & Consulting Services LLC was founded in 2005 in Stillwater, Minnesota. The firm serves clients nationwide, with a concentration in Minnesota and Wisconsin. Its service lines include Outsourced Accounting (account reconciliations, transaction entry into QBO, bank and credit card reconciliation, financial statement preparation, bill and payment processing, and expense/receipt tracking), Payroll, and Business Tax Services. Individual tax preparation is offered only internally, exclusively to owners of existing business clients, and is not promoted as a public-facing service. The firm is affiliated with the Minnesota Society of CPAs. Its brand tone is welcoming but professional, aiming to balance approachability with credibility.

## Positioning & Differentiation

Slachta positions itself as a trusted partner with niche-deep expertise — built for dentists, service businesses, and established entrepreneurs who want more than a tax preparer. Rather than competing on general tax-prep services, the firm emphasizes ongoing strategic guidance: cash flow management, budgeting, KPI tracking, retirement plan evaluation, and collaboration with clients' financial advisors on personal financial planning. Small business clients broadly are focused on expense management and cash flow, and the firm frames its value around addressing these operational financial concerns rather than compliance alone. The primary contact path is a form that segments visitors by individual vs. business owner and their area of interest, reflecting a client-type-driven intake approach.

## Industries Served

### Dentists

Dentists are named on the homepage as a client type the firm helps with "practical, strategic insight to keep you financially strong," and are reinforced in the ideal-client criteria on the Who We Serve page. This is an active, committed niche for the firm, though a dedicated dental page with specific pain points, KPIs, and proof points has not yet been built out.

### Service-Based Businesses

Service-based businesses are named on the homepage with a cash-flow and bill-pay angle, and reinforced in the ideal-client criteria on Who We Serve. The firm's stated idea clients under this umbrella include Insurance Agencies, Attorneys, Architects, and Specialty Healthcare Services. Common pain points for this group include cash flow management, budgeting, expense management, and bill pay. No dedicated pages or industry-specific proof points exist yet for these sub-types, but each is intended to receive its own dedicated page.

### Entrepreneurs / Established Small Businesses

This group is described generically as businesses the firm can "help take to the next level," serving as a broader catch-all beyond the named niches. No specific stage, size, or distinct pain points are defined for this segment beyond the general ideal-client profile, which includes needs around retirement plan evaluation, personal financial planning collaboration, cash flow management, budgeting, and KPIs.

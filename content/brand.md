# About Slachta Accounting & Consulting Services LLC

## Identity
Founded in 2005 and based in Stillwater, Minnesota, Slachta Accounting & Consulting Services LLC serves clients nationwide with a concentration in Minnesota and Wisconsin. The firm's services include outsourced accounting — reviewing account reconciliations, entering transactions into QBO, reconciling bank and credit card accounts, preparing financial statements, processing bills and payments, and tracking credit card expenses and receipts — along with payroll and business tax services. Individual tax preparation is offered only to owners of existing business clients and is not promoted as a public-facing service. Slachta is affiliated with the Minnesota Society of CPAs. The firm's tone is welcoming but professional.

## Positioning & Differentiation
Slachta positions itself as "a trusted partner with niche-deep expertise — built for dentists, service businesses, and established entrepreneurs who want more than a tax preparer." Rather than framing itself as a transactional tax-prep provider, the firm emphasizes ongoing strategic support: retirement plan evaluation, collaboration with clients' personal financial advisors on financial planning, cash flow management, budgeting, and KPI tracking. Its primary contact path — a form asking whether a visitor is an individual or business owner and what they're interested in — reflects this dual focus on business clients and the individuals behind them.

## Industries Served

### Dentists
Dentists are named on the homepage as a group the firm gives "practical, strategic insight to keep you financially strong," and are reinforced in the firm's ideal-client criteria. Specific dental pain points, KPIs, and proof points are not yet detailed on the site, though the niche is treated as an active area of focus.

### Service-Based Businesses
Service-based businesses are highlighted on the homepage around a cash-flow and bill-pay angle and are reinforced in the firm's ideal-client criteria. The firm's ideal-client list names specific types within this category: Insurance Agencies, Attorneys, Architects, and Specialty Healthcare Services. Across small businesses generally, common concerns include expense management and cash flow oversight.

### Entrepreneurs / Established Small Businesses
The firm describes its role for this group generically as helping "take your established business to the next level," without specifying business stage or size beyond the general ideal-client profile. Shared needs across this group include retirement plan evaluation, budgeting, KPI tracking, cash flow management, and collaboration with clients' outside financial advisors on personal financial planning.
